The generic-catch rule is a recommendation to catch specific exceptions *if possible*, not a proof that arbitrary callback/driver failures can safely escape. See the [CodeQL query documentation](https://codeql.github.com/codeql-query-help/csharp/cs-catch-of-all-exceptions/). Changing these failure-isolation or ownership contracts just to silence the recommendation would introduce a behavioral regression.
