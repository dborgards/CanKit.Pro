* fix(canopen)!: keep SdoTransferMode.Block at 3, and bound the expedited range by the threshold

Two review findings on the removal commit, both correct.

Renumbering Block 3 -> 1 to close the gap left by the removed members made
it reuse the value Expedited carried in 1.2.x. Removing the members gives a
recompiling caller a compile error, which is the loud failure this break

silent and remote. A gap in an enum costs nothing by comparison, so Block
keeps its value and the gap stays.

too. Three of the five affected doc sites already carried the qualification
